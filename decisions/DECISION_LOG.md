# Decision Log

This file records durable project decisions so future weekly work does not depend on chat history.

## 2026-08-25 — Repository becomes project source of truth

Decision: use `ScarletSkyFury-dev/allog-usa-market-watch` as the persistent source of truth for the weekly Allog USA Market Watch.

Reason: preserve editorial rules, source hierarchy, visual standards, recurring metrics, and lessons learned across ChatGPT/Codex sessions.

---

## Client-facing framing

Decision: Allog is the service provider responsible for managing delivery execution. The report must not tell clients to manage chassis, pre-pull, storage, detention, demurrage, appointments, empty returns, or similar operational details.

Use those factors only as market conditions Allog monitors when relevant.

Avoid unnecessary “extra cost” warnings; prefer language around flexibility, timing, congestion, availability, and predictability.

---

## Freshness over repetition

Decision: important recurring topics are not automatically repeated.

Examples:
- Supreme Court broker-liability decision
- ELP enforcement
- non-domiciled CDL
- ELD removals
- ILA/USMX contract
- holiday schedules

They return only when there is a fresh development, deadline, measurable effect, or current service consequence.

---

## Week-over-week compliance de-duplication

Decision: before drafting the compliance section, compare the proposed content against the prior approved edition.

If a rule, proposal, statistic, or explanation was already reported last week, do not repeat it at full length unless something materially changed.

Preferred treatment:
- lead with the genuinely new event or deadline;
- use one short background sentence only when needed for context;
- omit unchanged historical statistics and explanations;
- explicitly distinguish “still active this week” from “new this week.”

This is especially important for ELP, ELD, non-domiciled CDL, carrier shutdowns, and FMCSA rulemaking.

## Rail coverage

Decision: rail news is client-relevant only for service developments (new/expanded/suspended service, ramp changes, backlog/dwell, route changes, interruptions, labor/weather disruption).

Do not include rail fuel surcharges or internal rail pricing mechanics in the client-facing report.

## LMI Transportation Capacity

Decision: permanently monitor LMI Transportation Capacity as a recurring macro capacity indicator alongside diesel.

Method:
- latest monthly reading
- above 50 = expansion, below 50 = contraction
- MoM direction when updated
- YoY comparison when useful
- pair with Transportation Prices and current regional conditions

Never call it a drayage-specific index.

---

## LMI client-facing display

Decision: show **Transportation Capacity** and **Transportation Prices** as separate bullets, matching the visual style used for warehousing indicators.

Each bullet should include:
- latest value;
- prior-month value when available;
- whether the metric is in expansion or contraction;
- whether the direction tightened/eased or accelerated/slowed.

Do not leave the reader with an unexplained standalone index value.

---

## Warehousing LMI interpretation

Decision: when LMI is shown, always explain the diffusion-index threshold so clients understand values such as 46.3 or 75.5.

Preferred warehousing metrics:
- Capacity
- Utilization
- Prices

Translate into receiving flexibility, space availability, and short-term storage pricing.
