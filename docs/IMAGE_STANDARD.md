# Official Allog Weekly Image Standard

## Locked template

The official visual format is the Week 31/2026-style **Allog Boletim de Mercado** image. Future editions must preserve the same overall layout and proportions.

Recommended canvas: **4:3 landscape**, approximately **1408 × 1056 px**.

## Fixed layout

### Top band
- Dark navy background.
- Allog **US Domestics** logo block at upper left.
- Fixed horizontal logistics-photo strip across the top center.
- “your trust, our goal” block at upper right.

### Title band
- Large white title: `ALLOG BOLETIM DE MERCADO - SEMANA XX/2026`
- Blue date range below.
- Subtitle: `Atualização Semanal do Mercado Logístico dos EUA`

### Main left body
- White background.
- Heading: `PANORAMA DO MERCADO DE DIESEL DOS EUA`
- Subheading: `Preços em US$ por galão (diesel rodoviário, impostos incluídos)`
- Vertical national-average card on far left.
- National average in large type.
- WoW directional arrow and value.
- YoY directional arrow and value.
- Central U.S. map divided into PADD regions.
- California inset.
- Lowest-to-highest color scale at bottom.
- EIA source and reference week at lower left.

### Main right body
- Heading: `DESTAQUES DO MERCADO`
- Exactly **five** stacked highlights.
- Each highlight uses a navy circular icon, blue heading, and short client-friendly copy.

Default highlight families:
1. PREÇOS DO DIESEL
2. FLATBED / OPEN-DECK
3. WAREHOUSING / TRANSPORTATION
4. PORTOS / RAIL
5. COMPLIANCE

The exact topic may change if another current development is materially more important, but the right column remains five items.

## Color and typography rules

- Navy / white / bright blue primary palette.
- Orange/red for highest-cost geography and strong upward risk where appropriate.
- Green for favorable downward/positive directional arrows when semantically correct.
- Preserve the established typography hierarchy, spacing, and clean corporate appearance.

## Map rules

- PADD 1 = East Coast
- PADD 2 = Midwest
- PADD 3 = Gulf Coast
- PADD 4 = Rocky Mountain
- PADD 5 = West Coast
- Florida belongs to PADD 1.
- California gets a separate inset.
- Bottom line must identify lowest region and California/highest point.

## Weekly variable fields

Only these should routinely change:
- week number
- date range
- national diesel average
- national WoW
- national YoY
- PADD 1–5 values
- California value
- lowest region / highest point
- EIA reference date
- five right-side highlight texts

## Do not redesign

Do **not** add:
- extra data tables
- energy-watch bands
- footer slogans
- flags
- website/social bars
- alternate color palettes
- new dashboard sections
- additional side panels

The objective is weekly continuity and instant recognizability.

## Photo-strip quality rule

The logistics photo strip must look **photorealistic**.

Avoid:
- painterly textures
- repeated/blobby vegetation
- artificial “AI” surface texture
- pixelated-looking details
- duplicated objects
- warped trucks, ships, planes, trains, containers, cranes, or roads

Preferred imagery should resemble high-quality editorial/corporate photography: realistic lighting, natural materials, believable geometry, and clean photographic detail.

Suggested strip subjects:
- truck on highway
- container ship / ocean freight
- aircraft wing / air cargo context
- roadway / inland transportation
- rail containers
- port crane / terminal

## Final visual QA

Before approval verify:
- week number and date range are correct
- every diesel number matches the EIA table
- WoW arrow direction matches the sign
- YoY arrow direction matches the sign
- lowest region is correct
- California/highest point is correct
- source date is correct
- no stale highlight from the prior week remains
- all Portuguese text is legible and correctly spelled
- photo strip looks realistic
- layout still matches the locked official template
