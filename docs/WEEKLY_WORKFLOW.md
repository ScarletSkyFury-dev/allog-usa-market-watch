# Weekly Workflow

## Objective

Produce one current, client-relevant Allog USA Market Watch edition each week without recycling stale information or drifting from the approved visual/editorial standard.

## Recommended timing

The diesel map is finalized only after the weekly DOE/EIA diesel release is available. If EIA has not published yet, prepare the market narrative and hold the final image.

## Workflow

### 1. Research current week

Search for material developments in the last 7–14 days across:
- DOE/EIA diesel and energy direction
- flatbed/open-deck
- LMI transportation and warehousing metrics
- ports / terminals / drayage
- rail / intermodal service
- FMCSA / DOT / CVSA / Federal Register
- fraud / carrier-identity risk when current

Do not assume every section needs news.

### 2. Verify primary sources

For each selected story:
- locate the official source when one should exist
- verify effective date and whether it is still current
- distinguish final rules from proposals
- verify metric definitions
- confirm comparisons are like-for-like

### 3. Update diesel table

Capture:
- national average
- WoW
- YoY
- PADD 1–5
- California
- lowest region
- highest point
- reference week
- release date
- next release date

Do not generate the final image until this step is complete.

### 4. Update LMI capacity pulse

If a new monthly LMI has been published:
- update Transportation Capacity
- update Transportation Prices
- update Warehousing Capacity / Utilization / Prices
- record MoM direction
- add YoY comparison when useful

If no new LMI exists, carry forward the latest monthly value and state its reference month when needed.

### 5. Select weekly stories

Use the test:

**What changed + where + why it matters to the client?**

Prefer 1–2 concrete items over multiple generic warnings.

Examples of high-value items:
- port/terminal consolidation
- new rail route or frequency
- ramp closure/restriction
- confirmed backlog/dwell deterioration
- measurable flatbed rate/capacity movement
- warehouse-capacity contraction
- enforcement deadline that can remove usable drivers/carriers

### 6. Draft the email

Default sections:
1. Visão geral da semana
2. Diesel e mercado de energia
3. Flatbed / Open-Deck
4. Warehousing / Transload / Transportation Capacity
5. Portos / Rail / Drayage
6. Regulatório e Carrier Compliance
7. Conclusão

Shorten or omit a section when no material current development exists.

### 7. Editorial QA

Check:
- no stale holiday/event references
- no repetitive regulatory background without a new effect
- no competitor over-mention
- no internal operational checklist passed to clients
- no uncontextualized metrics
- no generic “monitor X” filler
- every key point has a client-facing consequence

### 8. Prepare image copy

Select exactly five short highlights from the approved email.

The image is a summary, not a second report. Use one or two short sentences per highlight.

### 9. Generate image from locked template

Read `docs/IMAGE_STANDARD.md` first.

Do not redesign. Confirm photo strip is photorealistic.

### 10. Final numeric QA

Cross-check image against the diesel data table:
- national average
- WoW / YoY
- each PADD
- California
- min/max
- reference date
- week/date range

### 11. Archive approved edition

Store approved text/data under:

`archive/YYYY/week-XX/`

Suggested files:
- `email.md`
- `diesel-data.md`
- `sources.md`
- `image-copy.md`

When the final image asset is available in the repository, save it as:
- `market-snapshot.png`

### 12. Update watchlists

After publication, carry forward only topics that warrant continued monitoring. A recurring topic must earn its place next week through a new development.
