## Source-use rules

- Never cite a stale holiday operating plan as current unless residual effects remain documented.
- Do not use a competitor’s market update as the only basis for a client-facing claim.
- Do not rely on social media alone for official closures, rules, price data, or enforcement deadlines.
- Record the publication/effective date for every weekly operational advisory.
